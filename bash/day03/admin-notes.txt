chmod = change rights for user, group & others
chown = change owner
ps = show active processes
top = active processes and systemload
kill = terminate a process SIGTERM, -9 kill a process immediately SIGKILL
systemctl = Systemcontroll shows Status of processes or more likely a systemd-unit if active, inactive or failed.
journalctl = Show system and process logs/systemd-journal
sudo = superuser do / substitute user do
